I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. Professor Junxian He also previously advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), from which I graduated in June 2024.

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. My research interests include:

* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
